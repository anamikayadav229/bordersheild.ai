About this Prototype
BorderShield AI is a Smart India Hackathon 2026 demonstration prototype. All identity data, document images, verification-database records and match results shown in this application are simulated for demonstration purposes. This system is not connected to the Ministry of Home Affairs, SSB, any government database, or any real border-control or immigration system.
